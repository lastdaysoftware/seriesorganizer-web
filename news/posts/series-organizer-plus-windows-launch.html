Series Organizer+ 1.0.0 is here for Windows

September 29, 2026

It's back! Series Organizer+ 1.0.0 is now available for Windows.

The idea that started with a simple TVShows.txt file. Several years later, Series Organizer+ brings that idea back with a fresh home for both movies and TV.

Everything you watch, together

Build your Library, keep track of watched movies and episodes, and jump back in with Up Next. Calendar helps you see what's coming, while Discover gives you new ways to search, browse and find recommendations shaped by your Library.

There's plenty to explore beyond the list, too:

  • Open title and episode details, cast biographies, artwork and videos.
  • Check where titles are available to watch in your selected region.
  • Follow matching TV and movie releases with the optional Release Feed.
  • Celebrate your watching milestones with Achievements.

Your Library is local-first. If you want, Google sign-in provides optional Cloud Backup and account syncing.

This is the beginning

Version 1.0.0 is the first public Windows release. We'll keep refining it as we hear what works and what could be better. The Android app is also in development, and will be available as soon as possible.
