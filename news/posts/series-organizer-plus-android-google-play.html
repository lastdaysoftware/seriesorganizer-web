SERIES ORGANIZER+ NEWS

Series Organizer+ is now available on Google Play

October 18, 2026

Series Organizer+ is now publicly available on Google Play! After internal testing, the Android app joins the public Windows release.

Your Library, wherever you go

  • Organize movies and TV shows together, with Detailed and Grid views, favorites and Archive.
  • Track movies, seasons and episodes, including your viewing history and what is Up Next.
  • Discover titles, browse cast and artwork, and explore trailers and viewing providers where available.
  • Follow upcoming releases in Calendar and choose episode notifications.

The Android app requires Android 10 or later. Google Play handles installation and app updates; availability depends on your device and region. The app helps you organize and discover titles, but does not stream full movies or episodes.

Local-first, with an optional connection

You can use your local Library without signing in. Optional Google sign-in enables account sync and Cloud Backup across supported Android and Windows devices.

Account sync exchanges supported Library changes and portable profile settings. After the initial manual sync succeeds, you can enable automatic checks while the app is active: Android checks in the foreground; Desktop checks while open, including in the tray. Conflicts still require review. Cloud Backup remains a separate, explicit backup and restore action.

Get started

Install Series Organizer+ from Google Play, or visit the download section for Android and Windows options. The Help section explains Library filters, sync, backups and the optional Windows Chrome companion.

Thanks to everyone who helped test the app and get it here. If you find an issue, contact support@seriesorganizer.com with your app version, device model, Android version and a short description. Never send passwords or authentication tokens.
