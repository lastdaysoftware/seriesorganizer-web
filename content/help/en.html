Quick answers

Do I need an account?

No. Your Library is local-first and works without sign-in. Sign in only if you want account sync or Cloud Backup.

Why are some titles missing?

Clear search and active filters, check Archive, and confirm the active profile. A title can also appear before its seasons and episodes finish downloading; use the Library update's Retry action if it failed.

Does automatic sync run when I close the app?

No. After a successful initial manual sync on each device, Enabled allows Desktop checks while the app is open, including in the tray, and Android checks while the app is in the foreground. Automatic sync is not an automatic cloud backup.

Can streaming mark something watched without asking?

No. Browser Watching sends a title for review. Recording a watch requires your explicit confirmation in Desktop.
