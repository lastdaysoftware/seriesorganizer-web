Series Organizer+ 2.1.0 is available for Windows. This release brings together the work since the public 1.1.0 update: new views, more useful filters, Browser Watching and stronger syncing. [Download the Windows installer](https://downloads.seriesorganizer.com/Series%20Organizer%20Plus-2.1.0.msi), or visit the [official download section](https://seriesorganizer.com/#download) for its size and SHA-256 checksum. The refreshed Android app remains in Google Play Internal testing, not public release.

**Update — 18 October 2026:** Android is now publicly available on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lastdaysoftware.seriesorganizer). Read the [Android launch announcement](https://seriesorganizer.com/news/posts/series-organizer-plus-android-google-play.html) for installation and getting-started information. References to internal testing below describe the status when this Windows release post was first published.

## Choose the view that fits

Library and Discover gain poster grids alongside their Detailed views. Up Next has compact cards with progress and the episode or movie date when available. The new filters make it easier to combine media type, favorites, archive and dates, with Your services available for your own Library titles on Library, Up Next and Calendar.

Browse and For You now skip titles you already own in your Library; Search still includes them. [See the new Android and Windows screenshots](https://seriesorganizer.com/#next-update).

## Browser Watching, with you in control

The Windows installer includes a companion Chrome extension for Netflix, HBO Max, Prime Video, Apple TV and Disney+. Pair it with Desktop on the same computer, send a detected title and review the match before recording a watch. Streaming or sending alone never marks anything seen.

**Update — 14 October 2026:** The companion is now published in the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ibcefjobpcadamdkbjhjgadakbmbpmob). Store installation is the recommended route; the bundled copy remains a manual fallback. The [installation and pairing instructions](https://seriesorganizer.com/guides/browser-watching-installation.html) also cover switching from an unpacked copy. The [Browser Watching guide](https://seriesorganizer.com/guides/browser-watching.html) covers sending, review, options, badges and troubleshooting.

## Less friction, stronger recovery

- New first-start setup for languages, region and services.
- Portable profile preferences in account sync and Cloud Backup.
- Enabled/Disabled automatic sync, grouped checks after edits and safer retries. Desktop checks while open, including in the tray; Android checks while in the foreground. Initial sync and conflict-review safeguards remain.
- Quicker matched-card actions and clearer unread/review filters in Desktop Releases, Local Files and Browser Watching.
- A selectable Windows installation folder, with verified app-data transfers and protection during upgrades and uninstall.
- Improved scroll behavior, Windows date/time handling, metadata refresh, provider matching and cross-device sync recovery.

Cloud Backup remains an explicit backup/restore action, separate from account sync. Automatic sync does not silently resolve conflicts and does not run after Desktop exits or Android leaves the foreground.

Keep a backup before upgrading. The Windows installer is unsigned, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning; download only from the official site. Android remains in internal testing.
