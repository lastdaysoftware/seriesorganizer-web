**Series Organizer+ is back!** With the Windows app already available and Android now in internal testing through Google Play, we're looking for people to help us put the next releases through their paces.

You don't need to be a developer. If you enjoy keeping track of films and TV shows, your everyday use and honest feedback can make a real difference.

## What can you test?

- **Android:** installation, browsing and managing your Library, marking movies and episodes as watched, and notifications.
- **Across devices:** optional Google sign-in, cloud backup and restore, and keeping your Android and Windows libraries in sync.
- **Windows:** the upcoming desktop release, including Local Files and Browser Watching with the companion Chrome extension.

Android testing requires **Android 10 or later**. The Chrome extension is still being tested and isn't available in the Chrome Web Store yet; we'll provide setup instructions if you'd like to try it.

## Want to join?

Email [support@seriesorganizer.com](mailto:support@seriesorganizer.com?subject=Series%20Organizer%2B%20testing) with the subject **Series Organizer+ testing**.

Tell us whether you'd like to test **Android, Windows, or both**, and include your device model and operating-system version. We'll reply with the access details and instructions for the relevant test build.

These are test builds, so please keep a backup of your Library before trying them. There's no need to send passwords, pairing codes or account credentials.

## What feedback helps?

Let us know what you were trying to do, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot or the app's exported diagnostic log can help when something goes wrong — please review either for personal information before sharing it by email.

Small annoyances, confusing wording and things that work well are useful feedback too. **Thank you for helping bring Series Organizer+ back!**
