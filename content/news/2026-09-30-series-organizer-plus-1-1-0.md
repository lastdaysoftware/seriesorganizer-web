**Series Organizer+ 1.1.0 is now available for Windows.** This update brings your Library closer to the way you actually watch, while making it easier to move between devices.

## Your files, your Library

The new **Local Files** view can scan a folder and its subfolders for movies and episodes, match them to titles in your Library, and open a file in your default Windows player. You choose the folder and scan interval, and you can hide the view from the sidebar when you don't need it.

## More personal, more flexible

- **For You** gives you more control over the Library title behind a “Because you watched” recommendation.
- The Library's **Up Next** preview can show different numbers of TV and movie cards.
- **Your Year** brings your viewing activity together in a year-by-year view.

## A smoother experience across devices

Account sync now handles Library additions and removals in both directions more reliably, with clearer review when device and cloud records differ. A progress indicator shows when newly synced titles are fetching their details. We've also fixed missing items in the Desktop navigation reorder settings and a layout issue in Profile > General > Navigation.

[Download Series Organizer+ 1.1.0 for Windows](https://seriesorganizer.com/#download) from the official website. Android remains in development.
